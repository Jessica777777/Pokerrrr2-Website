How to Play OFC Sit & Go — A New Format for Open Face Chinese Poker

Pokerrrr · Aug 4, 2026 · 2 min read

Same Cards, Real Skill

Every player in an OFC Sit & Go receives the exact same cards each round. The game rewards technique over luck — your edge comes entirely from how you read and arrange your hand. To keep it fair, every player's cards stay hidden as they place them, and all hands are revealed together at the end.

How OFC Sit & Go Works

Three players sit down and the game starts immediately. Each player starts with 300 chips, and the stakes climb as the match progresses:

HandsStakes
1–310
4–620
7–940
10–1260
13–1590
16–18120
19+900

Chip loss each round equals points lost multiplied by the current stake. The match ends once only one player has chips remaining, or after 30 hands — whichever comes first.

Fantasyland Rules

OFC Sit & Go follows standard Open Face Chinese Poker Fantasyland rules. If every surviving player qualifies for Fantasyland at the same time, the next round cancels Fantasyland for all of them — a Fantasy Land Chop.

More Ways to Play

OFC Sit & Go joins Regular, Pineapple, Progressive, and Joker as Open Face Chinese Poker formats, all available inside the OFC Poker & Blackjack 21 app.
