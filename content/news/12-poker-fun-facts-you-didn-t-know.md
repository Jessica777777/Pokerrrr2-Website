<p>In a game like poker with such a <a href="news/a-brief-history-of-poker.html">rich history</a> and so many classic stories and myths, there’s tons of interesting facts and tid-bits. Next time you sit down at the poker table, bust out some of these poker fun facts!</p>

<p>These are our top 12 interesting poker fun facts.</p>

<ol>
<li>When poker first started in the American South, players gambled for gold nuggets, dust, and coins. Eventually saloons invented chips to provide a standard unit.</li>
<li>Poker was originally played with 20 cards. The 52 card deck came about so that more people could play.</li>
<li>The longest ever continuous poker game lasted for eight years, five months, and three days at the Birdcage Theater in Tombstone, Arizona in 1881.</li>
<li>The longest ever continuous poker session by one player was <a href="news/poker-legends-phil-laak.html">Phil Laak's</a> 2010 marathon session of 115 hours straight at the Bellagio.</li>
<li>Poker player Andrei Karpov bet and lost his wife in a poker game. 	She willingly left him for the winner anyway.</li>
<li>In the history of the <a href="news/how-to-prepare-for-the-world-series-of-poker.html">World Series of Poker</a> (WSOP) Main Event, nobody has ever won the title with <a href="news/looking-at-pocket-aces-avoid-these-3-common-mistakes.html">pocket aces</a>. However, two runner-ups lost the final hand when their aces got cracked.</li>
<li>President Warren G. Harding lost all the White House china to General Douglas MacArthur’s wife, Louise, in a poker game.</li>
<li>President Richard Nixon was also an avid poker player. He purportedly used his poker winnings to fund his presidential campaign.</li>
<li>The world’s largest ever poker tournament was an online event with 253,692 players. An Austrian man won the whole thing after playing 470 hands.</li>
<li>Only four players have won back-to-back WSOP Main Event titles: <a href="news/poker-legends-johnny-moss.html">Johnny Moss</a>, <a href="news/poker-legends-doyle-brunson.html">Doyle Brunson</a>, <a href="news/poker-legends-stu-ungar.html">Stu Ungar</a>, and <a href="news/poker-legends-johnny-chan.html">Johnny Chan</a>.</li>
<li>Poker Legend <a href="news/poker-legends-phil-helmuth.html">Phil Hellmuth</a> holds the record for most WSOP bracelets: 16!</li>
<li>The biggest ever payout from a single poker tournament went to beloved pro <a href="news/poker-legends-antonio-esfandiari.html">Antonio Esfandiari</a> after his 2012 win of <a href="news/poker-stories-the-big-one-for-one-drop.html">The Big One for One Drop</a>. He took home nearly $18.35 million!
