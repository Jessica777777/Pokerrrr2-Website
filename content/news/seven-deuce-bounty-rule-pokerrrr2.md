<p>With Pokerrrr 2's <strong>Seven-Deuce Bounty Rule</strong>, playing 7-2 isn't just a meme—it's a mission. When enabled in private games, this rule rewards players who win a hand with 7-2 offsuit by collecting bounties from everyone else who played that hand. It turns every pot into a potential bluffing battle and injects high-stakes fun into every session.

<strong>How the Seven-Deuce Rule Works</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>If a player wins a pot as the sole winner while holding 7-2 offsuit (different suits), all players who didn’t fold preflop must pay a bounty to them.</li>
<li>If Run It Twice is enabled, the rule is not applied.</li>
<li>If multiple players hold 7-2 in the same hand, no one gets the bounty.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Why You Should Try It</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>More bluffing. Players try to win with the worst hand to trigger the bounty.</li>
<li>More fun. Friends start second-guessing every shove and showdown.</li>
<li>More action. Even weak hands become explosive.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Special Rule Cases – FAQ</strong></p>

<p>Q1: What happens if more than one player has 7-2?</p>

<p>A: The bounty does not apply. Only a solo 7-2 winner can collect it.</p>

<p>Q2: Can a player win just a side pot and still get the bounty?</p>

<p>A: Yes! If they win any pot alone while holding 7-2 offsuit, the bounty is paid.</p>

<p>Q3: Is the bounty applied when Run It Twice is on?</p>

<p>A: No. The rule is voided when Run It Twice is used, even if a player wins.</p>
