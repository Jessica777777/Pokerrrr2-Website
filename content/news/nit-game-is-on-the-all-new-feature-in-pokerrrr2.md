<blockquote><p>Win solo or pay the price — this isn’t your regular home game.</p></blockquote>

<p>Pokerrrr 2 has introduced a thrilling new game mode designed to shake up private tables and punish passive play: <strong>Nit Game</strong>. If you’re hosting a table with your friends and want to spice things up with real tension, strategic pressure, and no escape mechanics — <strong>this is your format.</strong></p>

<figure><img src="assets/images/news/nit-game-is-on-the-all-new-feature-in-pokerrrr2/01.jpg" alt="" width="1200" height="628" loading="lazy"></figure>

<p>What is Nit Game in Pokerrrr 2 ?</p>

<p>Nit Game activates when 5 or more players are dealt in at your private table. That round becomes a Nit Round, and from that point:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Only solo winners of the main pot</strong> earn a <strong>Nit Icon</strong> — no chopped pots or side pots count</li>
<li>The round continues until <strong>all but one player</strong> have claimed a Nit Icon</li>
<li>The <strong>last player without an Icon</strong> must pay <strong>every other player</strong> a Nit Value, set by the host</li>
<li>This penalty is taken from their stack, then their buy-in. If bankrupt, they enter <strong>reload mode</strong>
<br></li>
</ul>

<p>Why You Should Try Nit Game?</p>

<p>✅ Increases engagement and table intensity
✅ No "hit-and-run" players</p>

<p>✅ Builds rivalries and strategic pressure
✅ Great for regular private games with trusted friends
✅ Compatible with most fun rules (Run It Twice, 72, Bomb Pot)</p>

<p>How to Enable Nit Game?</p>

<p>When creating your private table, toggle on <strong>Nit Game</strong> in the setup menu. We recommend tables with 6+ players for maximum drama.</p>

<p>You can also adjust <strong>Nit Value</strong> between 1BB and 10BB.
