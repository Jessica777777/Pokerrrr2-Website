<p>YouTube star <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6v9lebOXb2Q" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>MrBeast</strong></a><strong> suffered a quick poker loss</strong> during his appearance on <strong>PokerAfterDark</strong>, where he famously joked, “I basically give away money for a living,”<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6v9lebOXb2Q" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> </a>Known for his outrageous stunts and massive giveaways, MrBeast brought his trademark humor to the poker table — but this time, the stakes were real.</p>

<p>His comment immediately caught the attention of poker legend <a href="news/poker-stories-phil-hellmuth-blow-ups.html"><strong>Phil Hellmuth</strong></a>, who joked, “Give away money!” while rubbing his hands together — the perfect setup for what would become <strong>MrBeast's viral poker game loss</strong>.</p>

<p><strong>MrBeast’s Big Poker TV Show Moment</strong></p>

<p>After showing up a couple hours late due to a delayed flight, MrBeast took his seat alongside poker legends <a href="news/poker-legends-phil-helmuth.html">Phil Hellmuth</a>, <a href="news/poker-legends-doyle-brunson.html">Doyle Brunson</a> and a cast of other characters including chess master Alexandra Botez and senator Ted Cruz.</p>

<p>He folds twice, saying that he wants to see how everyone else plays. “I usually lose, so it should be free money for you guys,” he explains.</p>

<p>Then he decides to limp in with 87o. Unfortunately for him, Hellmuth raises with aces. Everyone folds back around, and MrBeast calls.</p>

<p>“Beast vs. apex predator,” says Hellmuth with a smile.</p>

<p><a href="news/postflop-strategy-in-no-limit-hold-em.html">The flop</a> comes 29T, giving MrBeast an open ended straight draw. He leads out with a small bet, and Hellmuth instantly shoves his stack all-in.</p>

<p>Racked with indecision, MrBeast flips over his calls as he considers whether to call. Brunson is soaking it all in with a huge smile on his face.</p>

<p>“I mean, I feel like I’ll hit a straight,” reasons the YouTuber. “Let’s do it! Call.” He shoves his chips into the pot. “I just sat down. Let’s see what happens.”</p>

<p><strong>Beast Busted</strong></p>

<p>Hellmuth delivers the bad news and flips over the aces. “Of course!” exclaims MrBeast. “Well, I have plot armor at least. I know I’m going to win!”</p>

<p><a href="news/turn-strategy.html">The turn</a> is a king. He sits back in his chair. “The suspense…” he whispers.</p>

<p>The river brings a seven. “Ah, darn it. What’s the percentage chance that I hit it there?”</p>

<p>Doyle instantly says 2:1 as Hellmuth explains how to calculate the odds.</p>

<p>Thus ends MrBeasts big poker TV show moment. In a little less than seven minutes, he went from bankrolled to busted.</p>

<p><a href="http://busted.At" target="_blank" rel="noopener">At</a> least he was right about one thing. He definitely is good at giving away money!</p>
