<p>Here’s a fundamental truth of poker. You win the most chips when you get the most out of your value hands. Seems simple, right?</p>

<p>Well, you might be surprised just how often you could be leaving chips on the table. Have you ever been holding the nuts and decided to just put in a small <a href="news/don-t-be-afraid-to-value-bet.html">value bet</a> just because you’re desperate for a call? Sure, it may be easier to hero call a ½ pot or less bet on the river when you’re holding the top pair. But what about the times when they WOULD call that 2x or 3x pot bet?</p>

<p>Let’s say you’re holding the nuts on the <a href="news/how-to-play-the-river.html">river</a> in a small pot of only 8 big binds (Bbs). You know that you could get a call on a 2 BB bet most of the time. But what if you bet 16 or 24 BB instead?</p>

<p><strong>The Overbet Exploit</strong></p>

<p>Making this move will require a good read on your opponent. A lot of people will overfold to overbets, meaning that you can use this move on tight players as a bluff to get them to give up the pot more often than not. People will often fold top pair and middle pair to such a bluff.</p>

<p>On the other hand, if you’re playing against a more calling-station type player who can’t seem to find the fold button, there’s no reason not to go for the overbet when you’re holding a strong value hand. In this situation, we want to bet as much as we can get away with.</p>

<p>While it’s not always going to be the right time to make big overbets, it should be a part of your arsenal. You have to mix it up to be a winning poker player. So, when the time is right, dial it in and make the huge bet as an <a href="news/3-postflop-exploits.html">exploit</a>.</p>

<p>That’s how you get full value when you do get called. Don’t be afraid to go for you. You only need to get paid off a couple of times before you realize that there are few things better in life than winning the most chips possible with your premium hands.</p>

<p>So, next time you’re sitting on the river with <a href="news/poker-stories-three-way-all-in-with-the-nuts.html">the nuts</a>, try it out. You might just find yourself with a huge stack in no time.</p>
