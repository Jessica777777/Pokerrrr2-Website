<p>No matter where you’re at in your poker journey, there comes a point in which you’re just crushing the games you’re playing. If you’ve built up a big enough bankroll, it might be time to take a shot at some higher stakes!</p>

<p>In a recent<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yEw6ZYkntlo" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> </a><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yEw6ZYkntlo" target="_blank" rel="noopener">vlog by PokerfaceAsh</a>, we get to see this in action. At the 52 Social casino in Round Rock, Texas, she decides to buy into a $2/5 cash game for $1000, a bigger stack than normal in a bigger game than she’s used to.</p>

<p>It doesn’t take too long for her to get into the swing of things. Here’s what happened on the very first hand of the session.</p>

<p><strong>All the Action</strong></p>

<p>The action starts with an open raise from UTG +3, who is holding the KTd. Another active player flats behind with 97o, and another vlogger named<a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UClHTtNkaedA5x0aWedOnauw" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> </a><a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UClHTtNkaedA5x0aWedOnauw" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ryan Depaulo</a><a href="news/how-to-play-against-3-bets.html"> 3-bets</a> from the cutoff with A5c to $125. It then folds around to Ash in the big blind.</p>

<p>She looks down at<a href="news/how-to-play-pocket-jacks.html"> </a><a href="news/how-to-play-pocket-jacks.html">pocket jacks</a>: the biggest of the medium pairs. Deciding that she’s getting the right pot odds, she decides to call the $120 to go with JdJs.</p>

<p>The flop is a dream come true: AJ9 rainbow. Flopping middle set on an ace-high board is a great position, since it’s certainly possible that someone else could have flopped top pair and will pay off value.</p>

<p>Keeping her hand disguised, Ash is the first to check. The other plays think for a while before checking, and Ash is surprised that nobody decided to put in the<a href="news/c-bet-strategy-frequency-and-sizing.html"> </a><a href="news/c-bet-strategy-frequency-and-sizing.html">continuation bet</a>.</p>

<p>The turn is another juicy one: another 9. Now Ash is holding a full house, the second nuts, and she needs to ratchet up her value game, so she decides to make a smallish bet of $170, just a bit under half a pot bet.</p>

<p>Now it’s back on her opponent, the guy holding 97o, who can’t believe his luck: he just improved his bottom pair into a set! It doesn’t take long for him to snap off the all-in, and Ash can hardly contain her excitement as she makes the call.</p>

<p>Flipping up her hand, her opponent chuckles and says “very nice hand”. When the river brings a brick, a 3, Ash scoops up one of the biggest pots of her life, netting $662 after the very first hand.</p>

<p>When you go up in the stakes, you never know what could happen!</p>
