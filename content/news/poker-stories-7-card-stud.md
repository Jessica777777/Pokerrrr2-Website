<p>7-card stud may not grab the big headlines like No Limit Texas Hold’Em, but that doesn’t mean it doesn’t have a rich and<a href="news/the-history-of-7-card-stud-poker.html"> </a><a href="news/the-history-of-7-card-stud-poker.html">storied history</a>. In fact, before Hold’Em took over as the default game of choice, 7-card stud was one of the most popular poker variants around.</p>

<p>Poker legend<a href="news/poker-legends-phil-ivey.html"> </a><a href="news/poker-legends-phil-ivey.html">Phil Ivey</a> cut his teeth playing the game in his youth in Atlantic City, NJ. He used to grind the game as a teenager, and he even won his second and fourth World Series of Poker (WSOP) bracelets playing stud back in 2002.</p>

<p>In<a href="news/poker-legends-doyle-brunson.html"> </a><a href="news/poker-legends-doyle-brunson.html">Doyle Brunson's</a> seminal poker work<a href="http://files.pokerakademia.com/files/65/65150.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> </a><a href="http://files.pokerakademia.com/files/65/65150.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Super System</a>, he even devoted just as much space to 7-card stud as he did to Hold’Em. Even in Texas, this was a big game.</p>

<p>And of course, who could forget the<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cxwrUc4UPHc" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> </a><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cxwrUc4UPHc" target="_blank" rel="noopener">iconic Rounders scene</a> where Matt Damon schools his law professors in cards? You know what game they’re playing: 7-card stud.</p>

<p><strong>Daniel Negreanu’s Stud Final Table</strong></p>

<p>Another poker legend,<a href="news/poker-legends-daniel-negreanu.html"> </a><a href="news/poker-legends-daniel-negreanu.html">Kid Poker</a> himself, is also a big stud fan. On<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-j7xY6nlyQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> </a><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-j7xY6nlyQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener">his vlog</a>, he brings us right into the action. In one video, he’s at the final table of the $10,000 buy-in stud event at the 2019 WSOP. Though he eventually loses the bracelet heads-up against<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hennigan_(poker_player)" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> </a><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hennigan_(poker_player)" target="_blank" rel="noopener">John Hennigan</a>, he has some sick action to get there.</p>

<p>For instance, in one hand, Hennigan opens with Qc open. Negreanu looks down at 5sQh in the hole with a Qd door for a pair and decides to raise. He then gets cold re-raised by David Baker with the 4s. Hennigen folds and Negreanu calls, figuring Baker must have aces or kings.</p>

<p>4th street brings the 5c to Negreanu and the 10h to Baker. Baker makes the bet, and Negreanu calls, planning on raising on a bigger bet street and knowing that if he makes an open pair, he must be dead.</p>

<p>The next card is blank for Negreanu, but Baker lands the Ac. Although Negreanu is started to sweat it, he still calls the bet.</p>

<p>6th street gives a 2h to Negreanu and a 3h to Baker: all blanks, and they check. Finally, on the river, Baker checks and Negreanu takes a moment to think it over. He likes his hand but doesn’t want to get check-raised. After just checking it back, he flips his hand and wins the pot.</p>
