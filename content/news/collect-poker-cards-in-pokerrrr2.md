<p><strong>Play Poker to Earn Cards</strong></p>

<ol>
<li>Public Tables: Every 5 hands played in NLH, PLO, OFC, or Stud earns you 1 random card.</li>
<li>High Stakes Reward: Win a single hand with over 150BB at NLH Lv.3+ / PLO Lv.2+ / OFC / Stud (must see the flop) to earn 1 random card.</li>
<li>Rummy &amp; SNG: Complete 5 matches of Gin Rummy, Rummy, or SNG to get 1 random card.</li>
<li>MTT Bonus: Join 3 MTT tournaments to get 1 random card.</li>
</ol>
